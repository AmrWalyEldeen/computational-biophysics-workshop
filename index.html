What participants will practice

Scientific reasoning, not just software operation

The workshop emphasizes why each computational decision is made, how results should be checked, and how in silico findings can connect to instrumentation and wet-lab validation.

  • Prepare reproducible molecular systems and document assumptions.
  • Interpret docking, simulation, and free-energy outputs critically.
  • Create publication-ready analyses and scientific visualizations.
  • Connect computational predictions with experimental strategy.
